What is HEO Training?

A dedicated training platform for HEO players to practice zone prediction, rotations, positioning, routes and tactical decision-making.

How to Use

  • Open Zone Prediction to practice the 1st–5th zones.
  • Open Rotation Practice to mark routes, enemies, danger areas and team positions.
  • Use Move / Fix mode and map zoom/pan according to the exercise.

Benefits

  • Improve zone reading and rotation planning.
  • Practice team movement before tournament matches.
  • Build repeatable tactical decisions.
